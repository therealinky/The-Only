Example Tool A's maker announced, in a changelog post, that a live-preview panel is coming to its layout generator. The panel is meant to show a rendered preview next to the prompt field as you type, instead of requiring a separate "generate" step.

## What we know

The announcement describes the feature but doesn't include a public demo, and the maker hasn't said when it reaches general release. As of this writing, it's visible only to a closed group of testers.

## Why it matters for designers

If it ships as described, a live-preview panel would shorten the loop between describing a layout and seeing it — useful for early exploration, less useful once you're refining a specific direction. Worth watching, not yet worth planning around.
