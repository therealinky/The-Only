We ran a short, single-speaker social clip through Example Tool D to see how much of the rough-cut work it could take off our hands.

## What we did

We uploaded a two-minute unedited clip and asked for auto-captions plus a suggested rough cut trimmed for pacing.

## What worked

The suggested cut trimmed obvious dead air and false starts reasonably well. Caption timing was accurate for clearly enunciated speech.

## What didn't

Captions dropped or garbled words during faster, more casual speech, and needed a full manual pass before they were accurate enough to publish.

## Best for

A starting rough cut and a caption draft to correct — not a finished, accessible caption track on its own.
