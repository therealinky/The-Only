Example Tool B rewrote the section of its terms of service covering ownership of generated brand marks and wordmarks. We read the before-and-after terms so you don't have to.

## What changed

The prior terms were ambiguous about whether a generated logo belonged to the account holder or the vendor. The revised terms state plainly that the account holder owns the output, subject to the usual carve-outs for content that infringes someone else's existing trademark.

## Why it matters for designers

If you've used tools like this for early logo exploration and worried about ownership before handing final files to a client, this kind of clarification is worth reading closely — clause by clause, not just the announcement blog post summarizing it.

## What to watch out for

Terms of service can change again. Treat this as accurate as of the access date on our source, and re-check before relying on it for a specific client engagement.
