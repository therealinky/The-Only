Midjourney posted on October 8 that it's testing a Thinking mode for image generation. It isn't a new model or a new parameter. It's a second pass: you take a V8.2 image that came out close but wrong, and ask the model to look at what it made, find where it missed your prompt, and generate again.

## What changed

**Rerun (Thinking).** On the alpha site, open a V8.2 job in the lightbox and click Rerun (Thinking) in the lower-right corner. Per the changelog, it works on both standard and edit jobs. The model checks the image against your prompt for misses like wrong objects, layout, anatomy, or text, then makes a new attempt. Midjourney says its tests show gains in prompt accuracy, typography, and coherence. It calls this an early version and is asking for feedback in its Discord.

It's a rerun only for now. Midjourney says it might later ship as a broader thinking mode, or as a way to add more thinking to a job after it's finished.

**Shared folders.** The same update lets you share a folder by username, as a Collaborator (who can generate directly into it) or a Viewer (who can browse). You can also share by link, keep the folder private to the people you added, or open it so any Midjourney member can contribute. Images still live in each creator's own history, and an owner can delete a shared folder for everyone.

## Why it matters for designers

The most frustrating Midjourney result isn't a bad image. It's a good image with the wrong number of hands, a missing object from the brief, or a headline spelled almost right. Until now, the fix was rerolling and hoping, or rewriting the prompt. A rerun that starts from "what did I get wrong" targets exactly that near miss, and typography is the part illustrators and poster designers have struggled with most.

Shared folders matter for anyone doing concept work with other people. A moodboard or exploration round can now be one place the whole team generates into, instead of screenshots pasted into a chat.

## What to watch out for

This is an alpha test on alpha.midjourney.com, and Midjourney says plainly it's an early version. The improvements are Midjourney's own claims from internal tests, and we haven't tried it ourselves. Nothing yet says how much GPU time a Thinking rerun costs, so keep an eye on your usage if you lean on it.

On shared folders, read the privacy note in the sidebar before you put client concepts in one. A private folder isn't the same as Stealth mode.
